Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2025

Introduction

Arete AI ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you use our mobile application and website.

By using Arete AI, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide and improve our service:

  • Account information — When you create an account, we collect your name and email address.
  • Usage data — Tasks, routines, streaks, and goals you create within the app.
  • Conversation data — Messages you exchange with your AI coach, stored to provide context-aware coaching.
  • Device information — Device type, operating system version, and app version for support and analytics.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Arete AI service
  • Personalize your coaching experience
  • Send notifications and reminders you've configured
  • Respond to customer support requests
  • Monitor and analyze usage patterns to improve the app

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption. We use reputable cloud infrastructure providers and implement technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Conversation data with your AI coach is encrypted in transit and at rest.

We Do Not Sell Your Data

We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes. Your tasks, routines, goals, and conversations are private to you.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate Arete AI:

  • AI model providers — To power the coaching conversations. Conversation data is processed in accordance with their privacy policies.
  • Analytics — Aggregated, anonymized usage data to understand how the app is used.
  • Push notifications — To send reminders via Apple Push Notification Service (APNs).

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal reasons.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Export your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information below.

Children's Privacy

Arete AI is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the new policy in the app and updating the "Last updated" date above.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please reach out to us. We're committed to resolving any concerns promptly and transparently.
